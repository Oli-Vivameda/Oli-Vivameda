## Vivameda

Company intelligence built on a longitudinal workforce panel: how organisations were staffed, structured and skilled, year by year. 4M+ companies, 48M observed company-year records, 1950 to today.

We pre-register our studies, publish the aggregates, and keep the failures on record next to the results that held up.

| Repository | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [vivameda-research](https://github.com/Oli-Vivameda/vivameda-research) | **The core.** What the panel predicts and what it does not: acquisition, reversal and capability-turnover signals, plus the negative results |
| [vivameda-prediction-markets](https://github.com/Oli-Vivameda/vivameda-prediction-markets) | The same methods applied to short-horizon Polymarket crypto markets, run on paper with hash-pinned studies |
| [vivameda-blockchain](https://github.com/Oli-Vivameda/vivameda-blockchain) | Solana token discovery, on-chain safety screening and prospective outcome studies |
